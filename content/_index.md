## Assistant Professor and Extension Specialist {#assistant-professor-and-extension-specialist}

**(promotion to Associate Professor with tenure effective August 15, 2026)**

I am an agricultural economist who helps growers, industry groups, agencies, and research teams use economic evidence to make better decisions about specialty crops, from technology adoption to markets, supply chains, and policy.

I work in the [Department of Agricultural and Resource Economics at North Carolina State University](https://cals.ncsu.edu/agricultural-and-resource-economics). My program focuses on the economics of specialty crops (fruits, vegetables, nuts, and ornamental plants), with particular interest in perennial systems such as orchards and vineyards.

My research studies how specialty-crop producers respond to new technologies, varieties, markets, and policy. Much of this work focuses on perennial crops, where planting and replacement decisions shape production for many years. Recent projects examine disease-resistant varieties, orchard replanting, supply response, and controlled-environment agriculture.

Through my extension program, I provide economic analysis for specialty-crop producers and Extension agents across the Southeast. This includes enterprise budgets for strawberries, sweetpotatoes, and blackberries; market outlook presentations; and economic impact studies.

Recent projects include co-leading the [North Carolina Green Industry Impact Report](https://go.ncsu.edu/green-industry-impact), delivered to the state legislature, and contributing to the multi-state [Southern Regional Strawberry Plasticulture Production Guide](https://content.ces.ncsu.edu/southern-regional-strawberry-plasticulture-production-guide). I have reached more than 1,300 stakeholders through presentations across the region.

I received my Ph.D. and M.S. in Agricultural and Resource Economics from [UC Berkeley](https://are.berkeley.edu) and my Bachelor of Resource Economics from the [University of Sydney](http://sydney.edu.au). Before joining NC State, I was a postdoctoral researcher in Agricultural and Resource Economics at [UC Davis](https://are.ucdavis.edu), where I worked on interdisciplinary regulatory-impact studies for the California Department of Food and Agriculture.
